Four characters say it all

We put Taipei's two favourite late-night foods on one table: the garlic of the night-market fried chicken stall and the charcoal skewers of the izakaya.

蒜

Garlic

Garlic is the soul of Taiwanese fried chicken. A fistful goes into the fryer with every order, and our skewers get a shower of fresh-fried garlic crumble.

炭

Charcoal

Every skewer is grilled over charcoal, only after you order. Chicken skin is rendered slowly until it shatters.

夜

Night

Open until 1:30am every day, 2am on Friday and Saturday. Plenty of time after overtime, after the first bar, or after one more episode.

涼

Cool

No sweating in a sidewalk queue. Grab a number online and come over when your table is nearly ready.

    FAQ

    Do I need a reservation?

    Groups of up to 4 can walk in or join the online waitlist. For 5 or more, please book ahead so we can set up your table.

    Do you do takeaway or delivery?

    Everything on the menu is available to take away. For delivery, search “蒜串屋” on Uber Eats or foodpanda.

    How late are you open?

    Sunday to Thursday 17:30–01:30, Friday and Saturday 17:30–02:00. Last orders 30 minutes before closing.

    Is there a minimum spend or service charge?

    No minimum spend and no service charge.

    Do you have vegetarian options?

    Shiitake, corn, king oyster mushroom, green beans and sweet potato fries are meat-free. The fryer oil is shared with meat.

    Can we book the whole place?

    Yes — on weekdays, groups of 20 or more can book the restaurant. Call or message us first.
