Four stages of fire

上火Heat

We start only when the charcoal turns white. That is when the heat is steadiest.

翻面Turn

When to turn is read from the sound of the dripping fat and the colour of the meat.

離火Rest

Off the fire for a few seconds so the juices return to the centre.

上桌Serve

Served the moment it is ready, one skewer at a time. Nothing waits on the plate.

FAQ

Do I need to book?

Booking online is best, especially on Friday and Saturday nights. Walk-ins are welcome when there's a free table.

Can I order the course and à la carte together?

Yes. The course is 12 items; add any skewers or fried dishes after.

How late are you open?

Sunday to Thursday 17:30–01:30, Friday and Saturday 17:30–02:00. Last orders 30 minutes before closing.

Vegetarian or allergy options?

The seasonal vegetables can be made meat-free. Tell us about allergies when you book. The fryer oil is shared.

Good for a date or a birthday?

Yes. Add a note to your booking and we'll do what we can.

Can we book the whole place?

On weekdays, yes. Call us first.
